Why paying only the minimum is a trap

Your Chase Sapphire shows a minimum payment of $47. It feels manageable. But here is what actually happens if you only pay the minimum on a $1,200 balance at 24% APR.

If you pay minimum only 3.5 years to pay off · $612 in interest
If you pay full balance Done this month · $0 in interest

The minimum exists to benefit the bank, not you. Even paying $200 instead of $47 cuts the interest in half.
